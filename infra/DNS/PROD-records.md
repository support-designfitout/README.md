## Email Records

### MX Records (Mail Exchange)

MX records direct email to Google Workspace mail servers. These **must be DNS-only (not proxied)** in Cloudflare.

| Type | Name | Content | Priority | TTL | Proxied |
|------|------|---------|----------|-----|---------|
| MX | designfitout.com | aspmx.l.google.com | 1 | 3600 | ❌ No |
| MX | designfitout.com | alt1.aspmx.l.google.com | 5 | 3600 | ❌ No |
| MX | designfitout.com | alt2.aspmx.l.google.com | 5 | 3600 | ❌ No |
| MX | designfitout.com | alt3.aspmx.l.google.com | 10 | 3600 | ❌ No |
| MX | designfitout.com | alt4.aspmx.l.google.com | 10 | 3600 | ❌ No |

**Record Lines (CLI format):**
```
MX   designfitout.com   aspmx.l.google.com             1    3600  false
MX   designfitout.com   alt1.aspmx.l.google.com        5    3600  false
MX   designfitout.com   alt2.aspmx.l.google.com        5    3600  false
MX   designfitout.com   alt3.aspmx.l.google.com        10   3600  false
MX   designfitout.com   alt4.aspmx.l.google.com        10   3600  false
```

### SPF Record (Sender Policy Framework)

SPF prevents email spoofing by defining authorized mail servers. This **must be DNS-only (not proxied)**.

| Type | Name | Content | TTL | Proxied |
|------|------|---------|-----|---------|
| TXT | designfitout.com | `v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all` | 3600 | ❌ No |

**Record Line (CLI format):**
```
TXT  designfitout.com  "v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all"  3600  false
```

**Explanation:**
- `v=spf1` - SPF version 1
- `include:_spf.google.com` - Authorize Google Workspace mail servers
- `~all` - Soft fail for unauthorized servers (recommended for initial deployment)
- Alternative: `-all` for hard fail (strict enforcement)

### DMARC Record (Domain-based Message Authentication)

DMARC provides email authentication and reporting. This **must be DNS-only (not proxied)**.

| Type | Name | Content | TTL | Proxied |
|------|------|---------|-----|---------|
| TXT | _dmarc.designfitout.com | `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@designfitout.com` | 3600 | ❌ No |

**Record Line (CLI format):**
```
TXT  _dmarc.designfitout.com  "v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@designfitout.com"  3600  false
```

**Policy Evolution Strategy:**

1. **Phase 1 (Week 1-2): Monitoring**
   ```
   v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@designfitout.com
   ```
   - Monitor reports, no enforcement
   - Identify legitimate email sources

2. **Phase 2 (Week 3-4): Quarantine**
   ```
   v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=10; rua=mailto:dmarc@designfitout.com
   ```
   - Start with 10% quarantine
   - Gradually increase `pct` to 100

3. **Phase 3 (Week 5+): Enforcement**
   ```
   v=DMARC1; p=reject; rua=mailto:dmarc@designfitout.com; ruf=mailto:dmarc-forensic@designfitout.com
   ```
   - Full enforcement
   - Add forensic reports (`ruf`)

**Additional Options:**
- `sp=quarantine` - Subdomain policy
- `adkim=s` - Strict DKIM alignment
- `aspf=s` - Strict SPF alignment
- `pct=100` - Percentage of messages to apply policy to

### DKIM Records (DomainKeys Identified Mail)

DKIM records are generated by Google Workspace after enabling DKIM signing. These **must be DNS-only (not proxied)**.

**⚠️ IMPORTANT:** DKIM records are unique to your Google Workspace account and must be obtained from Google Admin Console.

**Placeholder Structure:**
| Type | Name | Content | TTL | Proxied |
|------|------|---------|-----|---------|
| TXT | google._domainkey.designfitout.com | `v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=<PUBLIC_KEY>` | 3600 | ❌ No |

**How to Generate DKIM Keys:**

1. Log in to [Google Admin Console](https://admin.google.com)
2. Navigate to **Apps → Google Workspace → Gmail → Authenticate email**
3. Click **Generate new record**
4. Select:
   - **DKIM key bit length:** 2048 bits (recommended)
   - **Prefix selector:** `google` (or custom selector)
5. Copy the TXT record value provided by Google
6. Add the TXT record to Cloudflare DNS

**Example DKIM Record (after generation):**
```
TXT  google._domainkey.designfitout.com  "v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=MIIBIjANBgkqhkiG9w0BAQEFAAOCAQ8AMIIBCgKCAQEA..."  3600  false
```

**Key Size Recommendations:**
- **2048 bits:** Recommended for security (default)
- **1024 bits:** Legacy, less secure (not recommended)

### MTA-STS Records (Mail Transfer Agent Strict Transport Security)

MTA-STS enforces TLS encryption for email delivery.

#### MTA-STS TXT Record

This announces MTA-STS policy availability. **Must be DNS-only (not proxied)**.

| Type | Name | Content | TTL | Proxied |
|------|------|---------|-----|---------|
| TXT | _mta-sts.designfitout.com | `v=STSv1; id=2025-11-09` | 3600 | ❌ No |

**Record Line (CLI format):**
```
TXT  _mta-sts.designfitout.com  "v=STSv1; id=2025-11-09"  3600  false
```

**Policy ID Guidelines:**
- Update `id` value whenever policy changes
- Format: `YYYY-MM-DD` or timestamp
- Purpose: Forces mail servers to fetch updated policy

#### MTA-STS Subdomain (A/CNAME Record)

The subdomain serves the MTA-STS policy file. Can be **proxied through Cloudflare** (recommended for DDoS protection).

| Type | Name | Content | TTL | Proxied |
|------|------|---------|-----|---------|
| A | mta-sts.designfitout.com | <Cloudflare_Pages_IP> | Auto | ✅ Yes |

**Alternative (CNAME):**
```
CNAME  mta-sts.designfitout.com  designfitout.com  Auto  true
```

**MTA-STS Policy File:**
- **Endpoint:** `https://mta-sts.designfitout.com/.well-known/mta-sts.txt`
- **Implementation:** Cloudflare Pages Function at `functions/.well-known/mta-sts.txt.ts`
- **Content-Type:** `text/plain`
- **Cache-Control:** `public, max-age=86400`

**Policy Content:**
```
version: STSv1
mode: enforce
mx: aspmx.l.google.com
mx: alt1.aspmx.l.google.com
mx: alt2.aspmx.l.google.com
mx: alt3.aspmx.l.google.com
mx: alt4.aspmx.l.google.com
max_age: 86400
```

## Cloudflare Proxy Recommendations

### ✅ Proxy Enabled (Orange Cloud)

**Should be proxied for:**
- Root domain (A record) - designfitout.com
- WWW subdomain (CNAME) - www.designfitout.com
- MTA-STS subdomain (A/CNAME) - mta-sts.designfitout.com

**Benefits:**
- DDoS protection
- CDN acceleration
- SSL/TLS management
- WAF protection

**Requirement:**
- Ensure Cloudflare Edge certificates are active
- Verify SSL/TLS mode is set to "Full (Strict)" or "Full"

---

### ❌ DNS-Only (Grey Cloud)

**Must be DNS-only for:**
- MX records (email delivery)
- SPF TXT record
- DMARC TXT record
- DKIM TXT records
- MTA-STS TXT record (_mta-sts)

**Reason:**
- Email protocols require direct DNS resolution
- Proxying email records will break mail delivery
- Email servers need to connect directly to Google's mail infrastructure

## SSL/TLS Configuration

### Cloudflare SSL/TLS Settings

**Recommended Configuration:**
- **SSL/TLS encryption mode:** Full (Strict)
- **Edge certificates:** Universal SSL (auto-provisioned)
- **Minimum TLS version:** TLS 1.2
- **TLS 1.3:** Enabled
- **Automatic HTTPS Rewrites:** Enabled
- **Always Use HTTPS:** Enabled

### MTA-STS Subdomain SSL

For `mta-sts.designfitout.com`:
- **Certificate:** Cloudflare Universal SSL (auto-provisioned)
- **Validity:** Must be valid and trusted (no self-signed)
- **Coverage:** Must include `mta-sts.designfitout.com` subdomain
- **Verification:** Use `openssl s_client -servername mta-sts.designfitout.com -connect mta-sts.designfitout.com:443`

## Troubleshooting

### Email Not Receiving

1. Verify MX records are set correctly and are DNS-only (not proxied)
2. Check SPF record includes `_spf.google.com`
3. Confirm Google Workspace domain is verified
4. Test with: `dig +short MX designfitout.com`

### DKIM Failures

1. Verify DKIM is enabled in Google Admin Console
2. Check DKIM TXT record is DNS-only (not proxied)
3. Verify public key matches Google's generated key
4. Allow 24-48 hours for propagation

### DMARC Reports Not Received

1. Verify `rua` email address exists (dmarc@designfitout.com)
2. Check DMARC record format is correct
3. Wait 24-48 hours for reports to arrive
4. Check spam/junk folder for report emails

### MTA-STS Policy Not Loading

1. Verify DNS record: `dig +short TXT _mta-sts.designfitout.com`
2. Test policy endpoint: `curl -I https://mta-sts.designfitout.com/.well-known/mta-sts.txt`
3. Check SSL certificate is valid
4. Verify Cloudflare Pages Function is deployed
5. Check Content-Type is `text/plain`
